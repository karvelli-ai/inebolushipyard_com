About INEBOLU SHIPYARD

A Legacy of Trust: A Karavelioglu Family Company

Our History

A Century of Engineering Excellence

INEBOLU SHIPYARD is more than just a maritime facility; it is a vital pillar of the KARAVELIOGLU COMPANIES GROUP, a family-owned industrial powerhouse with a history spanning over a century. Since the group’s foundation in 1916, the Karavelioglu name has stood for tradition, resilience, and engineering excellence.

Established in 2008 to bring this expertise to the Black Sea coast, Inebolu Shipyard operates as a family-run enterprise where long-term relationships and quality craftsmanship take priority. Being a member of a group with over 100 years of experience allows us to combine historical wisdom with modern naval technology.

Proven Industrial Track Record

Since our gates first opened, we have served the international maritime community with unwavering dedication.

Operational Excellence

We have successfully performed hundreds of vessel repairs, ensuring the safety and efficiency of the global merchant fleet.

Specialized Construction

Our portfolio includes the successful production of one bunker ship and two tugboats, demonstrating our ability to deliver high-performance, mission-critical vessels.
