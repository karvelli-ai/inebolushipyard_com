Repair & Maintenance

Rapid, reliable docking and technical services.

Overview

Minimizing Downtime, Maximizing Performance

In the demanding maritime industry, downtime is the enemy of profitability. INEBOLU SHIPYARD provides rapid, reliable, and high-quality repair and maintenance services designed to return your vessels to sea in peak condition.

As the only shipyard on Turkey’s Black Sea coast equipped with a floating dock, we offer a strategic advantage for vessels operating in the region, providing "all-in-one" technical support from emergency troubleshooting to scheduled class surveys.

Core Maintenance Services

Our facility is equipped to handle comprehensive lifecycle support for your fleet.

Dry-Docking & Hull Care

Utilizing our 4,500-ton lifting capacity floating dock, we perform high-pressure washing (up to 2,500 bar), grit blasting, and precision anti-fouling applications.

Mechanical & Engine Repairs

Comprehensive overhauling of main engines and auxiliaries, including turbocharger servicing, pump repairs, and gearbox maintenance.

Steel & Piping Works

Expert fabrication and renewal of mild steel, high-tensile steel, and stainless steel plates, with production rates optimized to minimize time in dock.

Propulsion & Steering

Specialized repairs for rudders, shafts, and propellers, including polishing and alignment services to enhance fuel economy.

Electrical & Automation

Troubleshooting and installation of navigation systems, communication equipment, and shipboard automation.
