Vessel Conversion & Retrofitting

Why build new when you can adapt?

Overview

Transforming Assets, Optimizing Value

INEBOLU SHIPYARD specializes in high-complexity vessel conversions that breathe new life into existing hulls. In an era of rapidly changing environmental regulations and shifting market demands, our conversion services provide a cost-effective and faster alternative to new construction.

From lengthening a hull to complete mission repurposing, we combine our "New Building" engineering expertise with our "Repair" speed to deliver a transformed asset that meets the highest modern standards.

Primary Conversion Services

We handle the most technically demanding modifications, ensuring structural integrity and class compliance at every step.

Mission Repurposing

Converting standard cargo vessels into specialized carriers (e.g., Livestock, Cement, or Bitumen carriers) or transforming OSVs into research platforms.

Hull Lengthening & Widening

Strategic structural modifications to increase cargo capacity or deck space, improving the vessel's earning potential.

Environmental Retrofits

  • BWTS Installation: Turnkey Ballast Water Treatment System integration.
  • Scrubber Systems: Exhaust gas cleaning systems for IMO sulfur cap compliance.

Propulsion Upgrades

Transitioning to hybrid systems, LNG-ready configurations, or high-efficiency propeller/nozzle upgrades.

Accommodation & Deck Upgrades

Modernizing living quarters or adding specialized heavy-lift cranes and equipment foundations.

The Inebolu Engineering Process

Conversion requires a deeper level of planning than standard repair. Our process utilizes advanced technology to minimize risk and downtime.

1

3D Laser Scanning

Digital twins for "perfect fit" pre-fabrication before arrival.

2

Naval Architecture

Recalculating stability, buoyancy, and load-bearing capacities.

3

Modular Fabrication

Parallel construction in specialized workshops to minimize downtime.

4

Class Supervision

Working with DNV, ABS, and Lloyd for guaranteed certification.
