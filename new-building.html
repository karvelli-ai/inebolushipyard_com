New Building & Naval Construction

Engineering visions into high-performance reality.

Overview

Transforming Vision into Reality

At INEBOLU SHIPYARD, we transform complex maritime visions into high-performance reality. Our New Building service offers a comprehensive, turnkey solution for shipowners and operators seeking vessels that don't just meet industry standards—they redefine them.

From the initial conceptual sketch at our facilities to the final sea trials, we manage every phase of construction with precision engineering and a deep-rooted commitment to quality craftsmanship.

Our Specialized Capabilities

We recognize that every mission is unique. INEBOLU SHIPYARD specializes in a diverse range of vessel types, ensuring your asset is optimized for its specific operational environment.

Commercial Cargo

High-efficiency bulk carriers, container ships, and tankers designed for maximum deadweight and optimized fuel economy.

Specialized Workboats

Rugged tugs, OSVs, and multicats engineered for reliability in the most demanding offshore conditions.

Research & Technical

Custom-built platforms equipped for subsea exploration, hydrographic surveying, and specialized environmental monitoring.

Passenger & Ferries

Modern, safe, and comfortable vessels focusing on efficient passenger flow and reduced environmental footprints.

The Inebolu Construction Journey

Phase Description
1. Design & Engineering Collaborative conceptualization, 3D modeling, and rigorous stability calculations.
2. Procurement Sourcing premium-grade steel and Tier-1 machinery to ensure vessel longevity.
3. Precision Fabrication Expert welding and modular assembly overseen by our senior naval architects.
4. Integration Seamless installation of advanced navigation, propulsion, and automation systems.
5. Commissioning Exhaustive sea trials and final certification by leading classification societies.
